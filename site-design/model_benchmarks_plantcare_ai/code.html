Known Operational Limitations

Empirical machine learning models are diagnostic accelerators, not absolute arbiters. The following boundary conditions govern automated decision-making.

BOUNDARY 01 CO-INFECTION

Multi-Pathogen Co-Infection Masking

When fungal blight and bacterial speck concurrently colonize a single leaf lamina, the model assigns priority weight to the primary necrotic zone (>60% surface area). Secondary opportunistic viral infestations are suppressed in top-1 inference. Agronomists must inspect margin contours manually.

FAIL-SAFE FLAG DUAL-LESION WARNING
BOUNDARY 02 ILLUMINANCE

Direct Midday Solar Glare > 240 RGB

Specular highlights induced by direct sunlight on waxy leaf cuticles (e.g., citrus or apple foliage) saturate RGB sensors above 240 intensity. This obliterates cellular color detail, triggering an automatic 'Specular Overexposure' rejection requiring angle re-orientation.

ACTION FORCE POLARIZED FILTER
BOUNDARY 03 ABIOTIC BIAS

Abiotic Stress vs Viral Chlorosis

Nitrogen starvation, iron deficiency chlorosis, and drought stress evoke spectral phenotypes identical to early-stage viral yellowing. Predictions with high abiotic probability require validation against paired soil electrical conductivity (EC) telemetry and rhizosphere moisture data.
