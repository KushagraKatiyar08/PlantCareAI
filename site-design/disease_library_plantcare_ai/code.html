PATHO-CLASS: Fungal Phytopathogen
INCUBATION: 48-72 HRS • VECTOR: AIRBORNE CONIDIA
biotech BIOLOGICAL SYMPTOMS & MORPHOMETRY

Concentric brown-black lesions with characteristic "target board" rings, bounded by chlorotic yellow halos. Defoliation accelerates under alternating wet-dry microclimates, progressively seizing lower senescent foliage first.

PATHO-CLASS: Oomycete Water Mold
INCUBATION: 24-48 HRS • HIGH MORBIDITY
biotech BIOLOGICAL SYMPTOMS & MORPHOMETRY

Rapidly spreading, water-soaked irregular dark green to purplish lesions on adaxial leaf surfaces. Under high relative humidity (>90%), delicate white velvety fungal mycelium and sporangiophores sporulate on abaxial surfaces.

Discrete, circular reddish-brown necrotic spots (2-10 mm) bordered by narrow dark brown bands. Tiny, raised coal-black specks (pycnidia) embed in ring formations within dead lamina tissue, inducing systemic fruit rot.
